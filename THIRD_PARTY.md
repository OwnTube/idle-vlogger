# Third-party material

- Original Lamar – Idle Vlogger assets and configuration were extracted from the user-provided APK. Their original ownership and terms remain unchanged. The Android executable and SDK libraries are not part of the browser payload.
- [PixiJS](https://github.com/pixijs/pixijs): MIT; installed through npm with its license.
- [pixi-spine](https://github.com/pixijs-userland/spine): runtime notices and Spine license are included by its npm packages. See `node_modules/pixi-spine/SPINE-LICENSE` and the runtime packages' notices after `npm ci`.
- [UnityPy](https://github.com/K0lb3/UnityPy): MIT; build-time resource reader.
- [Cpp2IL](https://github.com/SamboyCoding/Cpp2IL): build-time analysis/layout recovery tool, installed separately; no recovered method DLLs are shipped.
- [REA](https://github.com/morluto/rea), [Ghidra](https://github.com/NationalSecurityAgency/ghidra), [GhidraMCP](https://github.com/LaurieWired/GhidraMCP) and [ILSpy](https://github.com/icsharpcode/ILSpy) were used or evaluated as analysis tools. None is the browser runtime.
